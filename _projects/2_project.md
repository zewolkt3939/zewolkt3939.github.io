This controlled lab turns web-security concepts into repeatable exercises while strengthening Linux and service-administration habits.

## Practice areas

- Docker service setup, configuration, and troubleshooting
- Apache and WordPress deployment workflows
- Basic hardening checks, log review, and configuration awareness
- Safe reproduction of common web-vulnerability scenarios

## Working method

For each exercise, I identify the expected behavior, reproduce the issue in the lab, document the root cause and mitigation, and retest the change. All testing is limited to environments I own or am explicitly authorized to use.
