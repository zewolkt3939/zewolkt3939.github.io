I build repeatable networking scenarios to move beyond memorizing protocols and understand their behavior in practice.

## Topics practiced

- VLAN design and inter-VLAN routing
- STP/RSTP behavior, EtherChannel, and troubleshooting
- OSPF fundamentals, NAT, and ACL testing
- DHCP and DNS behavior in segmented networks

I validate configurations with packet-level observation tools and document what I see when a design works or fails.
