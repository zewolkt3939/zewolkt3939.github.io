I use a repeatable workflow to connect a vulnerability concept with its technical cause and an appropriate mitigation.

## Workflow

1. Understand the vulnerable behavior and its security impact.
2. Reproduce it safely in an authorized training environment.
3. Record the root cause, mitigation, and verification steps.
4. Retest to confirm the lesson and improve the notes.

## Topics in scope

SQL injection, XSS, CSRF, XXE, SSRF, authentication flaws, access control, business-logic issues, session management, OAuth, and JWT.
